## V1.2 — The setting everyone skips: Personal Preferences (6 min)

🟦 GAMMA OUTLINE
```
Title: The setting almost everyone skips
Slide: Universal settings = the layer that applies to EVERY conversation
Slide: 3 parts — About Me · How I Work · Communication Style
Slide: Paste the template → customise the brackets → save
Quote: "This is why Claude can feel like it doesn't quite get you."
```

NARRATION
> This next setting is the single biggest reason people feel like Claude "doesn't quite get them" after months of use. It's your Personal Preferences — and most people leave it blank.
>
> Think of it as the layer that applies to *every* conversation, before any specific context loads. Get it right once, and every future chat starts from a better baseline.
>
> Open Settings by clicking your name in the bottom-left. Go to General, find Personal Preferences, and paste in the template from the resource below this video. It has three parts: About Me, How I Work, and Communication Style.
>
> Now customise it. The About Me section has brackets — fill those with your real situation: where you are, what you do, your background, the tools you use. Don't dump your whole life story here; that comes in week two with Projects. This is just your working style and broad context.
>
> The How I Work and Communication Style sections are the magic — they tell Claude to cut the fluff, think in first principles, challenge your thinking, and be a thinking partner instead of a yes-machine. Save it, and feel the difference immediately.

## V1.4 — The 4 modes in 5 minutes (5 min)

🟦 GAMMA OUTLINE
```
Title: Claude isn't one tool — it's four
Four panels:
- Chat — your thinking partner, writer, researcher (Weeks 1–3)
- Projects — persistent context spaces (Week 2)
- Cowork — reads/writes files on your computer (Week 4)
- Code — build real software from plain English (Week 5)
Slide: Find each one now (sidebar + desktop top bar)
```

NARRATION
> Last thing this week: a five-minute orientation. Claude isn't one tool — it's four modes, each with a different job. Knowing the difference now means you won't be confused later.
>
> **Chat** is where you are right now — your thinking partner, writer, researcher, and strategist. This is what you'll use most in weeks one to three.
>
> **Projects** live inside Chat — persistent context spaces that hold custom instructions, documents, and memory for one area of your life. We build these in week two; for now, just find them in the left sidebar.
>
> **Cowork** is desktop-only — it reads and writes files on your actual computer, creates documents, works with your Drive and Notion. That's week four.
>
> And **Code** — also desktop — builds real software from plain English. That's week five, and it's nowhere near as scary as it sounds.
>
> Spend five minutes just locating all four. That's week one done — and you're already ahead of people who've used Claude for months.

[DEMO] Point to Projects in sidebar; open desktop app, point to Cowork and Code in the top bar.

✅ Actions: find Projects · Cowork · Code.
