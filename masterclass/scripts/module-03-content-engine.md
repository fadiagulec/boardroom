## V3.2 — Make it sound like YOU, not AI (5 min)

🟦 GAMMA OUTLINE
```
Title: Kill the "AI voice"
Slide: Feed Claude 3–5 of your best pieces
Slide: Have it extract YOUR voice rules (sentence length, words you avoid, rhythm)
Slide: Save those rules to the Project / a Skill
Slide: Few-shot beats describing — show, don't tell
```

NARRATION
> Here's the difference between content that works and content that gets ignored: it has to sound like you. The trick isn't describing your voice — it's *showing* it. This is called few-shot: give Claude three to five examples of your best writing and ask it to extract the rules — your sentence length, the words you never use, your rhythm, how you open and close.
>
> Then save those rules into your Project (or, as we'll see in week six, into a Skill). From now on, every draft starts in your voice. Watch the before and after — same topic, generic draft versus voice-matched draft.

[DEMO] Paste 3 writing samples → "Extract my voice rules" → save to Project instructions → regenerate the V3.1 draft → show before/after.

✅ Action: create and save your voice rules.

## V3.3 — Repurpose once, publish everywhere (5 min)

🟦 GAMMA OUTLINE
```
Title: One piece → a week of content
Slide: Long-form → LinkedIn post · X thread · email · carousel · captions
Slide: Each adapted to the platform, not copy-pasted
Slide: Specify format explicitly (bullets, hook, CTA)
```

NARRATION
> You don't need more ideas — you need to get more out of each one. Take one finished piece and have Claude reshape it for every platform: a LinkedIn post, an X thread, a short email, a carousel outline, captions. The key is to ask for each format explicitly — Claude can structure output as bullets, threads, a hook plus three paragraphs plus a CTA, whatever the platform needs. It's not copy-paste; it's adapted to each channel.

[DEMO] Take the V3.2 draft → "Turn this into a LinkedIn post, an X thread, and a short email — adapt to each platform." Show all three.

✅ Action: repurpose one piece into 3 formats.

## V3.4 — Your weekly content sprint (4 min)

🟦 GAMMA OUTLINE
```
Title: A repeatable weekly rhythm
Slide: 1) Batch angles  2) Draft in voice  3) Repurpose  4) Schedule
Slide: Save the whole flow so next week is faster
Slide: This becomes a Skill in Week 6
```

NARRATION
> Now make it a system. A weekly content sprint looks like this: batch a set of angles, draft them in your voice, repurpose each, and schedule. Once you've done it once and you're happy with the output, you'll save the whole workflow — and in week six, we turn it into a Skill that runs itself. For now, ship one piece of content this week, start to finish, inside your Project.

✅ Action: complete one full content sprint and publish one piece.
