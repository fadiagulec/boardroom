## V3.4 — Action 2, Part A: Brain dump + belief interview (5 min)

🟦 GAMMA OUTLINE
```
Title: The strategy needs YOUR soul, not a template
Slide: Brain dump — what you want to be known for, pillars, what you'll sell, your constraints
Slide: The belief interview (the positioning lives here):
 • known for in 3 years? • what you believe others disagree with?
 • what makes you angry about your industry? • what you learned the hard way? • what you never want to be associated with?
Slide: Vague answers = generic strategy. Be specific & opinionated.
```

NARRATION
> Now the part that makes your strategy *yours*. Before you touch Claude, get everything out of your head — a brain dump: what you want to be known for, the creators you admire and why, where your audience hangs out, your content pillars, what you might eventually sell, and honestly how much time you actually have. Voice notes, journal, whatever's fastest.
>
> Then the most important part: the belief interview. Five questions — what you want to be known for in three years; what you believe that most people in your space would disagree with; what makes you genuinely angry about your industry; what you've figured out the hard way that your audience hasn't; and what you never want to be associated with. Questions two, three and four are where your positioning lives. If your answers are vague, the strategy is generic — so be specific, honest, and opinionated.

## V3.5 — Action 2, Part B: Generate your strategy (6 min)

🟦 GAMMA OUTLINE
```
Title: Research + your beliefs → a full strategy brief
Slide: New chat — Opus, extended thinking ON
Slide: Paste brain dump + belief answers; Claude also reads your research doc
Slide: Out: positioning, signature beliefs, voice, content pillars, platform plan, funnel, monetisation
Slide: Challenge it like a strategist — don't just accept the report
```

NARRATION
> Now you combine the two. Open a new chat on Opus with extended thinking on. Paste your brain dump and belief answers, and Claude reads the audience research already in your Project. Then run the strategy prompt. The research provides the evidence; your answers provide the soul. What comes out is a ten-plus-page brand and content strategy — your positioning, signature beliefs, brand voice, content pillars, platform plan, a content quality checklist, lead-magnet and funnel direction, and monetisation.
>
> Don't just accept it. Question the decisions you don't agree with — ask why it chose a platform, why it structured your pillars that way. Be open to being challenged, but push back where it doesn't feel right. This should feel like working *with* a strategist, not receiving a report. Then save the final brief in your content Project.

## V3.6 — Action 3: Plan 30 days of content (6 min)

🟦 GAMMA OUTLINE
```
Title: Turn strategy into a 30-day plan
Slide: Load 3 things: audience research · strategy · your voice/style guide
Slide: Strategy-based plan — pillars, cadence, hooks, CTA, purpose (growth/authority/connection)
Slide: 3 different hooks per piece (verbal · on-screen · caption)
Slide: Inspiration-based — drop a raw idea, ask Claude where it fits
Slide: It's a draft, not a decree — edit to taste
```

NARRATION
> You've done the hard part. Now turn it into a plan. Make sure three things are loaded in your Project — your audience research, your strategy, and your voice from Week 2. Then run the 30-day plan prompt. For each piece it gives the pillar, the format, a specific angle, three hook options, the call-to-action, and the purpose — growth, authority, or connection — laid out as a table you can drop straight into Notion.
>
> There's a second way I use all month: inspiration-based. When an idea hits on a walk or in the shower, I voice-note it, drop it into the Project, and ask Claude how it connects to my strategy and which pillar it fits. The ideas come from your brain; the strategic thinking happens with Claude. And remember — the plan is a draft, not a decree. Edit it, move things, delete what doesn't feel right. Claude gives you a starting point built on research instead of random ideas; the taste is yours.
