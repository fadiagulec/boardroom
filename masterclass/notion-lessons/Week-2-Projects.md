## ▶️ Lesson 2.4 — The handover (never lose context)
*[ Paste your HeyGen video here ]*

**When a chat gets long, Claude "fills up."** Carry your context to a fresh chat in 30 seconds.

**📋 Copy this prompt when a chat gets long:**
```
This conversation is getting long. Before we continue in a new chat, write me
a handover document that covers:
1. Who I am — background, goals, working style, constraints
2. What we're working on — the full context
3. Decisions we've locked in (don't re-litigate unless I raise them)
4. Critical context a fresh Claude would miss
5. What comes next — current state + next steps
6. Open questions to pick up first
Write it so a fresh Claude can read it cold and be immediately useful.
```
Then paste the output into a new chat to continue.
