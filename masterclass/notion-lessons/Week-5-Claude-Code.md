## ▶️ Lesson 5.5 — Build it
*[ Paste your HeyGen video here ]*
**Do this:**
1. In Antigravity, switch to **Plan mode** (Shift+Tab), start on **Opus**.
2. Paste the **Build** prompt (📋 *Prompt Library → Week 5*) — it installs the frontend-design skill, reads your CLAUDE.md + PRD, and writes a plan.
