# Self-Paced Lesson Pages — how to use

These are your **student-facing lesson pages** (the self-paced format, like the Notion courses you've seen). One page per module; each lesson has a **video slot + the steps + prompts + checkboxes**.

## Your no-stress workflow (with HeyGen)
For each lesson:
1. **Record the screen** — open Claude and follow the numbered steps on the page (silent is fine; mistakes get trimmed). Use Loom or Screen Studio.
2. **Make the presenter** — paste the matching script from **`Built-in-a-Day-HEYGEN-SCRIPTS.docx`** (or `heygen-voiceover-scripts.md`) into **HeyGen** → generate your talking-head video.
3. **Combine** — put the HeyGen video over the screen recording (corner bubble or intro) in Descript/CapCut.
4. **Publish** — paste the finished video into the lesson page's *[ video ]* slot (Teachable or Notion).
