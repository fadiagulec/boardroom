# 30-Day Content Plan — aligned to *Built in a Day*
*A launch runway: build audience → earn trust → open the course. Every post is a free taste of a paid module.*

---

## The marketing approach (read first)

**The big idea:** your free content teaches the **WHAT** ("look what's possible, here's a quick win"); the course delivers the **HOW + done-with-you** (the full build, live calls, your scripts). Free content proves it; the course is how they do it themselves. Never give away the *whole* system for free — give wins, sell the path.

**Your unfair advantage = your story.** Lead with it relentlessly: *55, 30 years corporate, zero tech background, built a 300-vehicle fleet app in a day.* It removes the #1 objection ("I'm not technical / too old") before anyone raises it. Most AI creators are 25-year-old engineers — you are the proof that *normal people* can do this. Own that lane.

**The 3 content jobs (every post does one):**
- **Growth** — reach new people (hooks, contrarian takes, your story). ~50%
- **Authority** — build trust (teach a real win, show real Claude on screen). ~35%
- **Connection/Conversion** — deepen + sell (behind-the-scenes, offers). ~15%

**The CTA ladder (soft → hard) — this is the whole funnel:**
| Phase | Days | Goal | The ask (CTA) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Magnetise | 1–7 | Get found + followed | "Follow for the journey" · "Comment a word" |
| 2 · Teach | 8–17 | Build authority + a list | "Comment WORD for the free guide" → **waitlist** |
| 3 · Prove | 18–24 | Desire + waitlist | "Doors open soon — join the waitlist" |
| 4 · Launch | 25–30 | Sell | "Doors are open — link in bio" |

**Platforms (keep it simple):** pick **ONE primary** you'll actually post to daily — for your corporate audience that's **LinkedIn** *or* **Instagram**. Record once, repurpose to the other + a weekly **email/newsletter** (your conversion hub — that's where you sell). Don't spread thin.

**Format rule (your own Phase 3 method):** for every video, the **verbal hook**, the **on-screen text**, and the **caption first line** are three *different* angles on the same idea. Hooks create a curiosity gap or emotion in 3 seconds. Be specific: "I built a fleet app in a day" beats "AI is amazing."

**Proof > claims:** show the **real Claude screen** in as many posts as possible (same rule as the course). Screen-recordings of you actually doing it outperform talking-head tips every time.

## How to run it (so it's easy)
- **Batch on Sunday:** record the week's videos in one Loom/Screen Studio sitting (same setup as the course).
- **Repurpose:** 1 video → reel + carousel screenshots + caption + a line in the newsletter.
- **The newsletter is where you sell** — social gets attention, email closes. Put every "comment a word" lead onto the list.
- **Reuse course assets:** these posts are mini-versions of your module scripts — pull hooks/demos straight from `scripts/` and `prompt-library.md`. Free content and paid course stay perfectly aligned because they come from the same source.
- **After launch:** the same 30 days becomes your **evergreen library** — keep posting it on a loop; open the course every 4–6 weeks (or go always-on with a waitlist).
