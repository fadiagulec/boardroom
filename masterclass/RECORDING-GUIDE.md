# Recording Guide — Built in a Day (for your first time)
*Everything you need to film the course with Loom. Keep this open while you record.*

---

## 0) The mindset (read this first)
- **You do NOT record the whole course at once.** One short video at a time.
- **Perfect is not the goal.** If you fumble a line, pause, breathe, say it again — you'll trim it later. Re-recording a 3-minute clip is easy.
- **Your scripts are already written.** You're reading + showing, not making it up.
- **Start with ONE tiny video** to get comfortable (see "Your first 30 minutes" below).

## 2) Set up Loom (one time, ~10 min)
1. Go to **loom.com** → sign up (free to try).
2. **Download the desktop app** (not just the browser extension) — it records your screen + camera together more reliably. Install and sign in.
3. Open Loom. In the recorder panel choose:
   - **Screen + Camera** (so your face shows in a bubble over the screen)
   - **Camera:** your webcam · **Microphone:** your best mic (even AirPods beat a laptop mic)
   - **Screen:** "Full screen" (or the specific window)
   - Drag the **camera bubble** to a bottom corner; keep it small so it doesn't cover the demo.
4. Quick quality wins: face a **window or lamp** (light in front of you, not behind), close other apps, silence notifications (Do Not Disturb), tidy your browser tabs.

> ⚠️ **One thing to know:** Loom's **free** plan caps videos at **5 minutes**. Several lessons run longer. Two options: (a) upgrade to **Loom Business** (~$12–15/mo) for unlimited length, or (b) on free, **split** a long video into two parts. If you're on Mac and want a one-time purchase instead, **Screen Studio** does the same job beautifully.

## 3) How to record ONE video (the routine you repeat)
1. **Open the script** for the video (e.g. `module-01-foundation.md` → V1.1).
2. **Open what you'll show:** the Gamma slides for that video + the right Claude surface (the script tells you which — e.g. claude.ai for Weeks 1–3).
3. **Arrange the screen:** slides/Claude filling the screen, Loom camera bubble in the corner.
4. Click **Record** → 3-2-1 countdown.
5. **Title slide:** say the opening NARRATION lines, clicking through the slides as you talk.
6. **At [DEMO]:** switch to Claude and *do the action live* while narrating what you're clicking. This is the important part — real clicks, real output.
7. **End** with the ✅ action line ("pause and do this now…").
8. **Stop** the recording.
9. **Trim** the dead air at the start/end (Loom lets you trim in the browser).
10. **Download the MP4** and name it clearly: `M1-V1.1-get-set-up.mp4`. Drop it in a folder called `recordings/`.
11. Tick it off the list. Next video.

> You upload the MP4 files into **Teachable** later — record everything first, upload in one batch.

## 4) Your first 30 minutes (do this today)
Don't start with your story (that one matters most — save it for when you're warmed up). Start tiny:
1. **Warm-up (5 min):** record a 60-second test of yourself saying anything. Watch it back — check your face is lit, mic is clear, bubble isn't covering things. Delete it.
2. **First real video (10 min):** record **V1.3 — Privacy + your name**. It's only 2 minutes and it's pure click-along — the easiest win in the whole course.
3. **Watch it back (5 min).** You'll immediately feel more confident.
4. **Then do V1.1, V1.2, V1.4** to finish Week 1.

That's Week 1 filmed on day one. Momentum > perfection.

---

## 5) Smart order to record everything
Record by **surface**, so you're not constantly switching setups:
1. **Talking-head bits** (your story V0.1, intros) — camera-focused.
2. **Chat/claude.ai videos** — Modules 0–3 + Bonus B1–B8 (all live in the browser/desktop Chat).
3. **Cowork videos** — Module 4 (desktop app → Cowork).
4. **Claude Code videos** — Module 5 (Antigravity) + B7/B9/B10.

Batch a week per sitting. ~42 short videos sounds like a lot, but most are 2–6 minutes.

---

## 6) First-timer tips
- **Talk to one person**, not "an audience." Imagine explaining to a friend.
- **Mistakes are fine** — pause, then re-say the sentence. Trim later.
- **Don't script-read robotically** — glance at the NARRATION, then say it naturally in your words.
- **Energy slightly up** — on camera, "a bit much" feels normal to viewers.
- **Redact private info** on screen before recording (real names, emails) or blur in edit.
- **Stuck on tech?** Ask Claude — paste what you see. (That's literally the course's message.)

You've got this. If a 55-year-old with no tech background can build a fleet app in a day, you can record a 2-minute video. 🙂
